# Supply Chain Analytics & Optimization Dashboard

An interactive, enterprise-grade Business Intelligence solution developed in **Power BI Desktop** to diagnose supply chain throughput inefficiencies, analyze macro financial trajectories, and map high-risk compliance vulnerabilities.

---

## 🎯 Executive Summary
This dashboard translates a complex data ecosystem of **180,519 cloud-ingested records** into a highly scannable, executive-level diagnostic platform. The architecture is split across two interconnected reporting environments designed to streamline operational workflows and proactively curb financial capital leaks.

---

## 🛠️ Dashboard Architecture & Core Features

### 📦 Page 1: Operational Throughput & Financial Status
Focuses on facility stress testing, workforce alignment, and historical revenue tracking.
* **Core KPIs Summary:** Surfaces the global **On-Time Shipping Rate (OTSR) at 42.51%**, cumulative **Gross Revenue at \$12.10M**, and **Completed Orders at 59.491K**.
* **Workforce & Warehouse Throughput:** Relational density visualization isolates the **Fan Shop** department as the absolute facility bottleneck, sustaining a critical stress spike of **1,008 orders precisely at 10:00 AM**.
* **Financial Revenue Trajectory:** A chronological filled area chart tracking continuous cash flow expansion from a **\$343K** baseline (January 2015) up to the current **\$12.10M** accumulation.
* **Inferential Analytics Integration:** Textual framing maps the underlying Python data-science core, where a significant **Paired T-Test (T-Statistic: 92.9688 | P-Value: 0.0)** mathematically proves systemic carrier contract failures over random noise.

### 🚨 Page 2: Risk & Compliance Analytics
Functions as a dedicated compliance oversight environment to safeguard corporate revenue.
* **Risk KPIs Summary:** Tracks global security indicators including total **Fraud Orders (4K)**, the baseline average **Fraud Rate (2.25%)**, and total compromised **Fraud Sales (\$825.93K)**.
* **Fraud Risk Hotspot Detection:** Houses a high-contrast red-gradient categorical bar chart isolating the top 10 high-risk zones. It highlights the primary anomaly: the **Books category within the Pacific Asia market spiking at an alarming 8.25% localized fraud rate**.
* **Prescriptive Roadmap:** Features a natural language executive advisory column detailing critical interpretation and multi-tiered strategic suggestions (MFA deployment, dynamic risk-scoring engines, and carrier SLA realignments).

## 🎨 Advanced UI/UX & Technical Implementation
* **Seamless Navigation:** Implements a custom-coded Indigo/Violet bookmark navigation menu (`Risk View ➡️` and `⬅️ Operations`) to dynamically toggle views while maximizing title bar space.
* **Data Integrity Protection:** Customized **Edit Interactions** isolate the Top 10 High-Risk chart, ensuring drop-down dual slicers (`Department` and `Market`) do not warp global compliance baselines.
* **Design Standards:** Adheres to modern corporate layout practices utilizing uniform rounded-border containers (`8px`), a clear hierarchy of visual anchors via typography-scaled emojis, and precise data unit formatting (`K` vs. `M`) to guarantee readability.

## 📁 Technical Specifications
* **Software Environment:** Power BI Desktop (Optimized for 2026 corporate reporting standards)
* **Underlying Analytics Stack:** Google BigQuery Cloud Warehouse, Python 3.11 (Pandas, NumPy, SciPy, Seaborn)
* **File Format:** `.pbix` (Power BI Report Template)
